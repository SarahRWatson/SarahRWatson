I am currently working as a postdoctoral researcher at the [University of Reading](https://ourresearch.reading.ac.uk/met-spate/), working on solar wind data assimilation. I am a research scientist with a PhD in Solar and Terrestrial Physics, with expertise in data analysis, numerical modelling, remote sensing, and statistical analysis. I am experienced in solar wind modelling verification and analysis as well as operational space weather forecasting through my work at the British Geological Survey.
